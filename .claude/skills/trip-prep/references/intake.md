# Intake brief

Send this as one message. Drop any line the traveller profile already answers, and say so ("I've got your food and pace preferences from your profile"). Ask for answers in one reply, bullet points fine; anything left blank gets a stated default at the review.

---

**Trip**
1. Dates (first and last day), and flights or trains already booked — times and airports/stations.
2. Cities and how many nights in each, if decided. Hotels booked (name or link)?
3. Who's going this time, and anything new about them (mobility, kids, someone who doesn't eat X)?

**What you want from it**
4. Must-dos — places, foods, experiences you'd be sad to miss.
5. Things you'll do anyway that don't need planning (shopping districts, casinos, night markets, meeting friends) — I'll still mention the famous ones so nothing big is missed.
6. Days you already know are special: a friend's dinner, a rest day, an early flight.
7. Interests to weight the research: history, architecture, food, nature, shopping, nightlife, photography, museums.

**Food**
8. Anything different from your usual food preferences for this trip? Any meal you want to splurge on (and roughly how much)?

**Sources**
9. Share any saved posts or lists — Xiaohongshu, Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps or Amap lists, blogs. I can't open Xiaohongshu myself, so paste the share text or screenshots. Everything gets checked on a map or review site before it goes in.

**Practical**
10. Phone plan abroad (eSIM/roaming, does it include a VPN?) — this decides whether you can reach me and the Sheet while away.
11. Tickets you'd rather book yourself vs. ones you want reminders for (booking windows).
12. Anything from last trip you want done differently?

---

Defaults if not answered (state them at the review): pace and food from the profile; no two heavy days (day trips, altitude, big parks, travel days) in a row; one flex half-day every 3–4 days; an alternative for every meal and big stop; famous sights included with a one-line "what it is".
