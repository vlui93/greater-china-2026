# Checklist and packing starter

Generic items that held up on the first trip. Copy what applies into the trip's Checklist tab (`kind` task / pack / each), then add this trip's own items: booking windows, real-name tickets, altitude, weather by city. Packing that only one person needs stays on their phone (Prep → Packing → Just me).

## Tasks (kind: task)
**Documents & health**
- Passports valid 6+ months after the return date, for everyone.
- Travel insurance covering every country/region on the route; save the policy number and 24h line on each phone.
- Photos of passports and confirmations saved somewhere that works offline.
- GP visit if there's altitude or specific vaccines.
- Subscribe to the government travel advisory for each country.

**Phones & apps**
- eSIM or roaming for each region — and whether it includes a VPN (needed to reach Google and Claude from mainland China, and Claude from Hong Kong and Macau).
- Install map apps per region (Amap for the mainland, Google Maps elsewhere), the booking apps, and set up payment apps before arrival (e.g. Alipay with a foreign card, passport verified).
- Connect every phone to this app's Sheet and add it to the home screen, on wifi, before departure.

**Getting around & tickets**
- One task per booking window, due the day it opens (trains, timed-entry sights, popular restaurants).
- Check checked-bag allowances on domestic flights.
- Power banks: a printed 3C mark is required to fly within mainland China.

**Money**
- Tell the banks; two cards from different banks each; a little cash per region.

**Coming home**
- Customs and biosecurity for home: what food can't come back, what to declare.

## Group packing (kind: pack — one person brings it for everyone)
Plug adapters per region · multi-port USB charger · power bank (3C mark for the mainland) · first-aid kit (plasters, blister pads) · medicines (stomach, rehydration, antihistamine, painkillers, motion sickness) · hand sanitiser and wipes · sunscreen · compact umbrellas · laundry sheets · zip-lock and dry bags · foldable duffel for shopping.

## Everyone (kind: each — everyone packs their own; ticks stay on each phone)
Passport · two bank cards · phone, cable, earphones · own medicines in the carry-on · toiletries · a daypack · quick-dry tops and trousers for the number of days between laundry stops · a warm layer set if any stop is cold or high · rain jacket · walking shoes worn in, plus light shoes · cap and sunglasses · lip balm for dry or cold air.
