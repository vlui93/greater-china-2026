Trip Companion

Your trips, on your phone

This page ships empty on purpose — the repo it is served from is public. Every place, booking and day lives in your Google Sheet. Paste the web app URL and the API key once and the trip downloads to this phone, where it stays and keeps working with no signal.

Both are kept on this phone only, never in the repo. Setting up the Sheet takes about ten minutes and is written up in the project README.
